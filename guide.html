1. Install the app

Timetable is a web app: you add it to your Home Screen instead of downloading it from the App Store.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Safari and go to nadinehisham2-dev.github.io/Timetable-app.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen. If you see Open as Web App, keep it on. Tap Add.
  4. Open Timetable from the new icon. It opens full screen like a normal app.
Notifications only work when the app is opened from the Home Screen icon (iOS 16.4 or newer), not from a Safari tab.

Android or computer

Open the same address in Chrome. On Android use the menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen or Install app. On a computer it works in any modern browser.

After the first visit, the app also opens without internet. Changes made offline are kept on the device and sync when you are back online.

2. Account and syncing

When you open the app and you are not signed in, a welcome page appears with three choices:

  • Create account: type an email and a password (at least 6 characters). Your calendar then syncs automatically on every device where you sign in.
  • Sign in: use the same email and password on another device to see the same calendar.
  • Continue without an account: your calendar is saved only on this device. The welcome page shows again next time the app is opened, until you sign in.

Your email is only used as your sign-in name. The app cannot read your emails or access anything in your email account.

Sign in later, sign out, check sync

  • The small text at the top right shows the sync status: Sign in to sync →, Syncing…, ☁︎ Synced, or Offline · saved on this device or ⚠️ Not synced · tap for details. Tap it to sign in, see your account, or see what the problem is.
  • If you sign in on a device that already has its own plans, they are combined with your account; nothing is overwritten.
  • In the account window: Sync now or Sign out on this device (your data stays on the device).
  • Changes appear on your other devices within about 20 seconds, or straight away when the app is opened.

Forgot password

  1. On the welcome page tap Sign in, type your email, then tap Forgot password?
  2. Open the email you receive on the same device and tap the link (check spam if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes).
  3. The app opens on Set a new password. Type the new password twice and tap Save new password. You are then signed in.

Month view

Each day shows its plans: the place name in its colour with the time, and the tasks underneath. Ticked tasks are crossed out. If a day has more than fits, scroll inside that day. Friday and Saturday are shaded. The day you last opened has a thin red ring; today has a filled red circle.

Week view

Plans are shown as coloured blocks on an hourly timeline starting at 7am (earlier if a plan starts before 7am). Overlapping plans sit side by side. Dashed green lines show prayer times, labelled in the time column on the left. A red line marks the current time. Upright on a phone the whole day fits on screen; sideways, the page scrolls.

4. Places

A place is where a plan happens (for example Clinic, Uni, Gym). Each has its own colour.

  • Add a place: tap + after the place buttons, type a name, pick a colour (or ＋ for any colour), tap Add place. You can also choose + New place… in the Place list while adding a plan.
  • Rename, recolour or delete: ⚙️ → edit the names and colours → Save. Deleting a place that is used shows a warning first; its plans then show as "Other".
  • Filter: tap a place button to show only that place; tap more to add them; tap All to show everything. While a filter is on, an orange bar says Showing only … with a Show all button. Each device remembers its own filter.

5. Adding plans

  1. Tap any day in the calendar (or a day header / empty column in Week view).
  2. The day opens with its prayer times and plans. Tap + Add.
  3. Fill in what you need:
    • Place (required).
    • Start and End times (optional; plans without a time appear in the "all-day" row in Week view).
    • Tasks: one per line. Each task can have its own optional time. + Add task adds another; − removes one.
    • Notes (optional).
    • Reminder and Repeat (see below).
  4. Tap Save or Done. Both save.

If the new plan overlaps another one, a ⚠️ warning names the clash. Change the time, or tap Save anyway.

6. Quick add

Tap ＋ Quick add and type the plan in any order. A preview shows how it was understood before you tap Add (or Edit details to open the full form).

Tasks are separated with commas. A place name that doesn't exist yet becomes a new place.

7. Editing, long press and deleting

  • Edit: open the day and tap anywhere on the place card. Change anything and tap Save or Done.
  • Long press: press and hold a place card for about half a second to open a menu:
    • 📝 View notes: read or change the notes.
    • 🔔 Set reminder: choose a reminder time.
    • ↻ Repeat: set or change how it repeats.
    • ⧉ Copy to… and ➜ Move to…: put it on another day.
    • 🗑 Delete: remove it (with a confirmation).
  • Undo: almost every change shows a message with Undo for 8 seconds, and ↶ at the top undoes changes at any time while the app is open.

8. Tasks and unfinished tasks

  • Tick a task by tapping its checkbox in the day view (tapping the task's words opens editing instead). Ticked tasks are crossed out in Month and Week views.
  • Tasks can have their own time, shown before the task (e.g. "11am Dina diagnosis").
  • ↺ unfinished at the top lists tasks not ticked on earlier days (last 30 days). For each one: tick it, → Today to move it to today, or ✕ to remove it from the list. Tasks from repeating plans are hidden there unless you turn on Include tasks from repeating items.

9. Repeating plans

In the plan's Repeat option (or long press → Repeat):

  • Every day
  • Every week / every few weeks on…: pick the days, and choose every week, every other week (one yes, one no), every 3 or every 4 weeks.
  • Every month (same date)
  • Every month on certain weeks: pick 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th or Last, and the day (e.g. 1st & 3rd Sunday).
  • Until: an optional end date.

Changing or deleting a repeating plan

The app asks which days the change is for:

  • This day only
  • This day and the days after it (past days stay as they were)
  • Choose dates… (only between a From and To date; leave To empty for "onwards")
  • All days, past included

To add a repeating plan to a day it normally isn't on, open that day and tap it under Add a repeating item to this day only.

10. Copy, move and overlaps

  1. Edit the plan (or long press) and choose Copy to… or Move to….
  2. Pick the date. The app shows what will happen and warns if it overlaps something.
  3. Tap Yes, copy/move to …. An Undo button appears afterwards.

For a repeating plan, only the chosen day is copied or moved; the rest of the series stays.

11. Templates

  • Save a template: plan a typical day, open it, tap Save day as template, give it a name.
  • Use a template: open any day, tap 📋 Use a template, choose one. Its plans are added as normal plans that can still be edited one by one.
  • Delete templates in ⚙️ Settings.

12. Reminders and notifications

Setting reminders

  • One plan: in its Edit screen choose Reminder, or long press → Set reminder.
  • Many plans at once: 🔔 → Set reminders for (all places or one place) → choose the time → Apply. It also applies to new plans added to that place.
  • Choices: at start time, 5/10/15/30 min, 1–2 hours, 1–2 days, 1 week before, or Custom… (any number of minutes, hours, days or weeks). Plans without a start time use 8am.

Turning on notifications (once on each device)

  1. Sign in (notifications need an account).
  2. On iPhone/iPad, open the app from its Home Screen icon.
  3. Tap 🔔 → Turn on notifications → Allow. A test notification ("✅ Notifications work") should arrive within seconds.
  4. The box shows whether automatic checks are running. Use Send a test any time; Turn off here stops them on that device.

Notifications arrive within about a minute of the set time, even when the app is closed, and always use your latest times. The device needs internet at that moment.

Without notifications turned on, reminders still pop up inside the app while it is open.

13. Prayer times

  • Prayer times are for Alexandria, Egypt, calculated with the Egyptian General Authority of Survey method. They update every day and follow Egypt's summer and winter time automatically. They may differ by a minute or two from a local mosque.
  • Shown in each day's details, in the Week view (dashed lines), and the next prayer in the top bar.
  • Prayer reminders: 🔔 → Prayer reminders. For each prayer:
    • At prayer time: at adhan, or 5–30 minutes before.
    • Before the next prayer: a second reminder before that prayer's time ends (Fajr → before Sunrise, Dhuhr → before Asr, Asr → before Maghrib, Maghrib → before Isha, Isha → before next Fajr).
    Copy Fajr's setting to all copies both, then tap Save prayer reminders.

14. Search and sharing

  • Search (🔍): type a name, task, place or note. Results show the date (or the next date for repeating plans). Tap one to open that day.
  • Share (📤): choose dates (this/next week, this/next month, or custom), places, and whether to include tasks and notes. Then Copy text (for WhatsApp or messages) or Save as image. It is a snapshot; later changes don't update it.

15. Settings, undo and backup

  • ⚙️ Settings: places and colours, text size (Small, Medium, Large, Extra large; saved per device), templates, account and backup.
  • Download backup / Restore backup: saves all your plans to a file you keep, or restores from one. Useful for keeping a monthly copy or moving to another account.
  • Undo/redo: ↶ ↷ at the top. The history lasts while the app stays open.
  • Tour: the first time the app opens, a short tour explains the screen. Replay it from ? → Show the tour again.

16. Troubleshooting

Notifications don't arrive

  1. Make sure the app was opened from the Home Screen icon and you are signed in.
  2. 🔔 → check the box says notifications are on, then tap Send a test.
  3. On iPhone/iPad check Settings → Notifications → Timetable is allowed, and Focus / Do Not Disturb isn't hiding them.
  4. Make sure the plan actually has a reminder (Edit → Reminder) or prayer reminders are saved.
  5. If the test says automatic checks aren't running, the app owner needs to check the reminder service.

My calendar isn't the same on two devices

  • Both devices must show ☁︎ Synced and be signed in with the same email.
  • Tap the sync text → Sync now. The account window says when it last synced, or what went wrong (for example no internet, sign-in expired, or the sync service can't be reached from this network; then try mobile data or another Wi-Fi).
  • If one device was used without an account, its plans stay on that device. Use ⚙️ → Download backup there and Restore backup on the signed-in device.

Other common problems

  • A plan shows on one device but not the other: check for the orange Showing only … bar (a place filter is on). Tap Show all.
  • The welcome page keeps appearing: this is expected while you are not signed in; it shows each time the app is reopened.
  • Something was deleted by mistake: tap ↶ (while the app is still open), or restore a backup.
  • Text is too small: ⚙️ → Text size → Large or Extra large. On a phone you can also turn it sideways.
  • A day doesn't show all its plans: scroll inside that day's box, or tap the day.
  • Forgot password email doesn't arrive: check spam and wait a few minutes; requests are limited to a few per hour.
  • The page is blank or frozen: fully close the app and reopen it. If it continues, use ? → Ask Claude and describe exactly what you see.

17. Privacy

  • Without an account, everything stays on your device.
  • With an account, your timetable is stored securely so it can sync; only you can see it.
  • Your email is only your sign-in name. The app cannot read your emails or access your email account.
  • Prayer times are calculated on the device; your location is not used.
