# Timetable-app

A monthly calendar planner. Each day shows your places (each with its own color), times and tasks. Tap a day to see details, add, edit or delete.

- Week starts on Saturday; Fri & Sat are shaded
- Manage places & colors with the 🎨 button
- Data is saved on your device; use **Back up data** under 🎨 to keep a copy

On iPhone/iPad: open the page in Safari, then Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
