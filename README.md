# Timetable-app

A monthly calendar planner. Each day shows your places (each with its own color), times and tasks.

**Features**
- Month and week views (week starts Saturday; Fri & Sat shaded), swipe to change month/week
- Tap a day for details: tick off tasks, add, edit, delete
- Repeating items (daily / weekly on chosen days / monthly), with "this day only" changes, removals and additions
- Copy or move an item to another day, with a confirmation step, overlap warning and Undo
- Filter by place, search across all months
- Reminders per item, or for all items / one place at once; export to iPhone/iPad Calendar (.ics) for alerts when the app is closed
- Prayer times for Alexandria (Egyptian General Authority method), adjusting daily and for summer/winter time
- Syncs across devices when opened as a Claude page; otherwise saved on the device. Backup/restore under 🎨
